CONFIGURATION

Settings & rates

Set the rates and wage ceilings used by future calculations.

Statutory caution These values are editable because rates, ceilings and applicability can change. Verify them against current EPFO notifications and your establishment's applicable rules before generating an ECR.

Changes apply to new calculations only. Existing monthly records retain their saved figures.

Calculation logic

  • MPF = PF wages × MPF rate.
  • If pension member: EPS = min(Pension wages, EPS ceiling) × pension rate; CPF = MPF − EPS.
  • If not a pension member: CPF = PF wages × non-pension CPF rate; EPS = ₹0.
  • EDLI = min(PF wages, EDLI wage cap) × EDLI rate.
  • Admin charges = PF wages × admin charge rate.

Amounts are rounded to two decimal places. If EPS exceeds MPF, the record is flagged and must be reviewed.
